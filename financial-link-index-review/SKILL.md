# Financial link-index review

1. Inventory every URL in the supplied index, including follow-up posts and linked long-form reports; assign source IDs and track opened, readable, partial, or blocked so coverage is measurable. Treat index titles as navigation rather than evidence. For Investor Day or long-range guidance requests, follow references/investor-day.md to discover official materials, resolve fiscal dates, and separate new targets from reaffirmed outlooks.
2. Read applicable source-specific skills before retrieval; use substack-reader for Substack and browser-automation for browser work. Fetch independent public posts with web_fetch, preserving each URL and returned text. Inspect returned truncated flags and spill paths rather than assuming maxChars guarantees complete content. Read a returned spill file with read when needed, because the displayed extraction may omit the end.
3. Open long-form articles with browser and inspect the visible ending as well as the beginning; record the exact section where a subscription gate begins, because a successful HTTP response does not prove full access. Use bounded browser text extraction or read-only DOM slices for long visible text. Stop at access gates and label unavailable sections without subscribing or bypassing them.
4. For attached PDFs and datacenter capacity or policy-risk reports, follow references/pdf-capacity-policy.md to verify full-page coverage, inspect charts, and separate forecast capacity from policy exposure and actual delays. Inspect financial charts as well as prose; extract image URLs from the visible article, load permitted image URLs or downloaded images with view_image, and record units, periods, scenario labels, and footnotes. Compare the latest prose against chart labels and earlier posts, because revised text may leave stale charts in place. Keep an explicit unresolved discrepancy when no reconciliation is supported. For supplier matrices or requests to record each company’s supply chain, follow references/supply-chain-matrices.md to preserve every cell and create company-level retrieval entries.
5. Follow issuer or SEC links to primary filings and locate the actual financial tables; verify revenue, operating income, net income, cash flow, balance sheet, and non-GAAP reconciliation rather than relying on search snippets. Label interim and quarterly tables unaudited when the filing does. Preserve filing URL and table/page locators for each group of figures. For release-time earnings reviews, use references/live-earnings.md alongside the earnings-review skill to verify call timing, cash-flow signs, changed segment scopes, and timestamped valuation inputs. For earnings-call reviews or follow-ups to an initial release review, follow references/earnings-call-followup.md to retrieve complete Q&A and record what the call resolves, changes or leaves undisclosed.
6. Build a figure ledger with metric, value, currency/unit, period, source ID, locator, and classification: reported fact, management outlook, author estimate, or own calculation. Keep revenue run-rate separate from recognized revenue, commitments separate from expenses and cash payments, and undrawn conditional facilities separate from cash. Read references/accounting-checks.md before interpreting margins, liquidity, buybacks, or shareholder-return scale; use its capital-return steps to resolve the plan scope and compare annual payouts against market capitalization.
7. Reconcile each revised figure to its latest supported definition; check whether a percentage is quarterly or year-to-date, whether a subtotal is already included, and whether different commitment totals share the same measurement date and scope. Do not fill blank filing fields with estimates without labeling them.
8. Present financial facts first, author forecasts with assumptions second, and company vision and concerns separately from your investment interpretation; retain material contradictions and unavailable details. Use Chinese when the request is Chinese and bullets rather than Markdown tables in Discord. For reusable data, produce CSV with explicit units and periods and verify row widths and arithmetic before delivery.
9. Deliver artifacts through an available attachment mechanism and verify delivery; do not present local filesystem paths as clickable downloads in a remote chat. Report the actual read coverage and access limits with the result.
10. When asked to save the review in Obsidian for later retrieval, inspect the configured vault protocol and existing company page before writing; merge a dated research entry rather than duplicating or replacing historical research. Create a company-level navigation page if absent, linking the full report, source snapshots, financial CSVs, and evidence records with vault-relative links. Keep the information cutoff, actual-versus-forecast labels, unresolved discrepancies, and follow-up checklist on that entry page so later retrieval does not turn estimates into facts.
11. Refresh the configured research index after archival and run an entity-name search that returns the new page; confirm both the saved note and successful retrieval before claiming it is ready for future use. Use the helper's documented arguments, inspecting its help before adding pagination flags. State the vault-relative note name in the completion message rather than offering a local filesystem URL as a remote download.

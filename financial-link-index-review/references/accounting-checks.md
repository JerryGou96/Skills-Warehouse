# Accounting checks for research summaries

1. Recompute gross margin as (revenue minus functional cost of revenue) divided by revenue; identify where channel fees, free-tier inference, training, and internal workloads are classified, because a high reported gross margin may exclude material economic costs. Never interpret marketplace revenue share as the channel commission rate.
2. Reconcile GAAP operating income to adjusted operating income and GAAP net income to adjusted net income using the filing adjustments; distinguish noncash fair-value changes from cash burn while retaining dilution and financing obligations as economic risks. Do not assume adding back SBC alone reproduces the company definition.
3. Keep expenses by nature and expenses by function as alternative views of the same total; compare their totals instead of summing them, because compute and research figures may overlap.
4. Calculate simple FCF as operating cash flow minus cash purchases of property and equipment; separately show finance-lease principal and working-capital effects, because simple FCF can omit substantial equipment funding cash outflows. Do not classify purchases of short-term securities as operating burn or equipment capex.
5. Separate cash, short-term investments, restricted cash, undrawn facilities, and forecast IPO proceeds; preserve draw conditions and use restrictions because these are not interchangeable sources of liquidity. Distinguish fair-value debt carrying amounts from contractual cash principal due.
6. Record each compute contract with counterparty, minimum or maximum amount, term, delivery trigger, cancellation conditions, interest treatment, and inclusion in any subtotal; do not sum overlapping arrangements or different reporting-date totals. Avoid dividing current cash by lifetime commitments as a runway estimate.
7. Extract forecast growth, financing, lease-principal, and spending assumptions from scenario footnotes; label projected negative cash as an unmet financing need, because it is not a feasible continuing cash balance. Keep year-end annualized revenue separate from annual recognized revenue.
8. Attribute supplier revenue-recognition claims to their source and require the supplier’s own disclosures to verify them; a lessee finance lease does not by itself establish the lessor’s sales-type classification.
9. Treat new-model revenue concentration as evidence of rapid replacement, not proof of training payback; require model-level costs and contribution cash flows before claiming ROI. Treat internal usage valued at list prices as an operational metric, not external revenue or equivalent cash savings.
10. Distinguish economic-impact estimates from company addressable revenue, personal equity pledges from corporate spending, and donated usage-credit face value from cash cost, because each measures a different economic quantity.
11. For screenshot-derived buyback comparisons, use web_search with issuer name, announcement period, and “share repurchase” or “shareholder returns”, then web_fetch the issuer announcement; verify the underlying amount before comparing, because a screenshot may combine dividends, repurchases, and employee compensation. If an investor-relations page is unreadable, search the issuer’s official newsroom for the same release and verify its date and text.
12. Split capital-return figures into new authorization, remaining authorization, announced purchases, completed purchases, and shares designated for cancellation versus employee compensation; retain the execution horizon, because an authorization is not cash already spent and employee awards can offset share-count reduction. Keep annual shareholder-return targets separate from a single board resolution and avoid adding previously announced amounts already included in the target.
13. Resolve the scope of a follow-up before correcting units: identify whether the user means the annual shareholder-return envelope, the latest buyback tranche, or employee stock allocation, and restate that scope because similar numbers can refer to different pools. Show local units and dollar equivalents explicitly; ask one short clarification when the reference remains ambiguous rather than assuming the employee allocation.
14. Reconcile the annual envelope to its components before describing allocation; label “at least” amounts as lower bounds, mark completed tranches as included, and leave any unreconciled balance unallocated because approximate totals and component floors do not establish a separate spending bucket.
15. Convert local currency using an explicitly dated or screenshot-supplied exchange rate and show local_amount / local_currency_per_USD; retrieve market capitalization with web_fetch, retain its displayed as-of date and retrieval time, and compute annual_dividends_plus_buybacks / market_cap because absolute dollar size obscures relative scale. Prefer same-date local-currency market cap for the domestic issuer; label mixed-date or reference-FX calculations approximate.
16. Compare annual capital returns with annual capital returns, keeping multi-year authorizations separate; retrieve issuer annual reports or cash-flow tables with web_search and web_fetch, record fiscal end dates, and prefer matching trailing periods because plans and historical execution are not equivalent. If only different complete fiscal years are verified, label the result a scale comparison rather than a same-period ranking.
17. Reconcile reported repurchase activity with cash-flow repurchase payments before selecting a peer numerator; use the issuer table over an inconsistent secondary summary and state the chosen basis because settlement timing and presentation can produce different totals. Read the relevant table from a returned spill file with read when web_fetch truncates it, and verify column headers before adding dividends and repurchases.
18. Lead the result with “每100元市值对应约X元年度分红加回购”, then separate dividend and buyback components because the combined ratio is not a cash dividend yield. Explain that completed payments are not all available to a new buyer, employee issuance can offset repurchases, dividends adjust the share price, and special investment-gain distributions may not recur; assess net share-count change and recurring free-cash-flow coverage before calling the program sustainable.

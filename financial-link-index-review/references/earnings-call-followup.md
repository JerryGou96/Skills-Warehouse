# Earnings-call follow-up

1. Read the earlier release-only note and any pre-call question checklist before retrieving the call; preserve their timestamps because later commentary supplements rather than rewrites the initial assessment. Organize the follow-up around the same questions and mark each answered, partially answered or unanswered, because the user needs to see whether the call resolved the original investment concerns.
2. Search company IR for the dated call and remarks, then open https://www.marketbeat.com/earnings/reports/YYYY-M-D-company-slug-stock/ and search company + quarter + fiscal year + earnings call transcript; inspect speaker text rather than AI-generated takeaways because summaries can overstate claims.
3. Read truncated web_fetch output through its returned spill path with read; verify the operator opening, CFO outlook, analyst exchanges and closing remarks because the displayed response may omit Q&A. Record speaker and timestamp locators for consequential claims.
4. Cross-check ambiguous amounts against another transcript or issuer filing; preserve preliminary-transcript labels and do not count syndicated copies as independent confirmation because they can share transcription errors.
5. Build a delta ledger with prior unknown, management answer, locator and remaining gap; classify guidance as maintained, raised, lowered or deferred because repeating an existing range is not an increase. Label annual guidance minus year-to-date actuals as an implied remaining-quarter calculation, not separate quarterly guidance.
6. Reconcile cash-flow explanations to the release: separate fixed-asset capex, inventory and other working capital, one-time payments and financing; label exclusions as your sensitivity calculation rather than issuer adjusted FCF because these affect different statement lines. Show whether excluding a one-time payment actually changes FCF's sign.
7. Trace hardware-system sales through contract, backlog, inventory procurement, delivery, revenue recognition and collection; distinguish this from assets retained to provide cloud services because hardware revenue can increase segment growth while inventory consumes operating cash. Preserve undisclosed hardware revenue, margins and ex-hardware growth as gaps rather than estimating them from segment totals.
8. Separate historical margins from forward cost timing, including third-party bridging capacity, depreciation and power; identify when new costs begin affecting results because reported margins may precede announced headwinds.
   - For factory outages or other exceptional events, record lost revenue, margin-point impact, write-off timing and expected shipment recovery separately; calculate guidance excluding the stated impact only as a sensitivity because an incident may explain only part of the deterioration and lost shipments can persist after write-offs stop.
   - Separate inventory correction from end-market consumption and component availability when discussing recovery; use explicit Q&A clarifications over broad optimism because easier comparisons or completed destocking do not establish that supply shortages or demand weakness have ended.
   - Map supply constraints by process node, product, packaging material and delivery period; retain simultaneous shortage and underutilization where management describes them because capacity freed in one product cannot necessarily serve another without conversion time and complementary supplies.
   - For semiconductor yield follow-ups, search the saved transcript for the exact technology name and read surrounding speaker exchanges with timestamps; separate wafer yield, substrate yield, packaging yield, material availability and cycle time because a packaging supply shortage is not evidence of a wafer-process defect.
   - Preserve technology specificity when reading family-level commentary: distinguish EMIB from EMIB-T and distinguish process-node variants; attribute a failure to a specific variant only when the source does, because a general progress statement does not identify the affected technology or root cause.
   - Record absolute yield, relative yield improvement, percentage-point change, unit-cost reduction and utilization as separate metrics; leave undisclosed absolute yields unknown because cost reductions also reflect cycle time, scale and product mix. Treat generic engineering failure modes as explanatory examples, not issuer-confirmed diagnoses.
   - Test yield improvement against product-level profitability and factory loading: identify which products are margin-accretive and which remain below the corporate average, because better technical yield can coexist with lower consolidated margins when output or utilization falls.
   - When explaining guidance operating leverage, calculate midpoint revenue times the same-basis gross margin, then subtract same-basis operating expenses; label the result an approximate implied operating profit and margin, not standalone company guidance, because rounding and unmodeled items prevent a precise EPS bridge. Keep interest, tax, non-controlling interests and diluted shares separate from that operating calculation.
   - When capex is below plan, record whether management attributes the difference to efficiency or delayed delivery and map the catch-up period to calendar dates; avoid calling it a spending peak because deferred capacity can raise later spending. Keep cash PP&E, lease commencements and reported capex distinct rather than forcing an unverified arithmetic bridge.
   - Decompose backlog into total growth, weighted duration and the portion recognized within twelve months; compare near-term growth with total growth because long-dated contracts can inflate the headline without matching next-year revenue growth. Keep bookings flow separate from backlog stock and note large-contract comparison effects.
   - Label adoption metrics by unit: daily users, paid seats, credit-consuming customers, consumption and revenue; record any refusal to update totals because usage acceleration is not a disclosed revenue growth rate.
   - Extract the assumptions behind cloud guidance, including utilization gains, incremental engineering efficiencies, model mix, revenue per token and new capacity delivery; preserve management uncertainty because growth dependent on these levers is not simply contracted demand becoming revenue.
9. Record analyst, firm, question, answer and unanswered quantitative request for material exchanges; distinguish management-attributed causes from inference because a confirmed monetization drag does not explain all revenue deceleration. Treat numbers embedded in analyst questions as assumptions unless management adopts them.
   - Separate explanations of year-over-year earnings changes from explanations of the current consensus miss, because a prior-year investment gain can explain declining EPS without explaining why analysts overestimated this quarter.
   - For foundry progress, distinguish internal transfers, recurring external production revenue and non-recurring customer programs; track PDK release, evaluation, tape-out, committed orders and volume production separately because revenue growth and customer interest do not by themselves establish a durable external manufacturing business.
   - Capture direct answers about next-year free cash flow even when no formal annual guidance is issued; distinguish management’s qualitative expectation from an analyst’s proposed capex amount because a negative cash-flow outlook can materially change an otherwise positive growth assessment.
   - Label incremental margin or fall-through ranges as incremental economics rather than corporate margin targets, and distinguish purchase-order growth from capex growth because these percentages have different denominators and timing.
   - Preserve the exact comparison behind event-timing basis points, including shifts between quarters and changes in year-over-year growth; do not add a sequential growth-rate effect to the reported growth rate as an adjusted result because the denominator may differ. Test the explanation against management’s next-quarter recovery forecast rather than declaring the miss entirely temporary.
   - Match customer populations and periods before relating token growth to spending growth, because a selected enterprise cohort’s spending and platform-wide token volume cannot establish a change in average token price.
   - When a cloud provider plans external chip sales, record the launch timing, manufacturing allocation constraint and internal-versus-external revenue-recognition trade-off, because the same scarce chips cannot simultaneously support both revenue scenarios. Attribute claims about lifetime returns or competitor cost disadvantages to management unless independently verified.
10. Audit liquidity and commitment definitions: distinguish marketable equities from cash, multi-year maximum commitments from current debt or annual capex, and contracted inventory from risk-free collection because headline totals omit timing and counterparty risk.
11. Refresh the quote for the same share class and retain its timestamp; keep the initial consensus provider or label a different provider because switching share classes or consensus sets creates artificial changes in expectations gaps.
12. Deliver a Chinese Discord-friendly delta review: conclusion, brief actual-versus-expectations baseline, guidance changes, newly answered business and cash-flow questions, unanswered Q&A, timestamped reaction and confirmation criteria. Save and link the call note alongside the initial note because later retrieval needs the evidence behind revised or unchanged interpretations.
